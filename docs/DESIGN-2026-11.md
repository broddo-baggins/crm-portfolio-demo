# Design direction, November 2026 (v1.2 and v1.3)

**Reading this as:** a B2B sales workspace for sales managers and the recruiters who open this portfolio demo, with a calm, dense, opinionated product language, built on the existing Thermal tokens (Tailwind, Radix/shadcn customised, no new system).

Dials: VARIANCE 5, MOTION 4, DENSITY 7. This is product UI, so the landing-page rules of the taste skill apply only where they carry over: no AI-purple, no glows, no em dashes, one accent, one radius scale, real states.

## Audit of v1.1.1 (screenshots `before-*`)

- It is clean but generic: white cards with a title, a number and an icon chip on every screen, the same card repeated 20 times. Hierarchy comes from nothing but size.
- Dashboard KPIs carry a trend percentage and no shape. Hot leads is a plain table with no face, no heat and no conversation, although the product is a WhatsApp conversation.
- The sidebar is a flat list of 10 links plus a stats block, always 248 px wide, with no grouping and no shortcuts. Search is an ordinary input with a dropdown.
- Leads and Messages pages open with four stat cards that push the real work (the table, the chat) below the fold at 800 px. Chat bubbles look like a form, not WhatsApp.
- Numbers use the body face. Figures do not align in columns.
- On 375 px the page works but a hamburger is the only way around the app.

## Direction

1. **Type with a voice.** Geist for text, Geist Mono for every figure that sits in a column or a KPI (tabular, slightly tight), Rubik for Hebrew. Display figures at 30 px, tracking -0.03em. Headings stay 600. Section labels are sentence case, never tracked caps.
2. **Ember earns its place.** Ember marks exactly four things: the primary action, the active nav item, the focus ring and heat (hot leads, the hot slice of a chart). Everything else is ink and steel. Data series: ember, steel, amber, moss, pale steel, all checked in both themes.
3. **Hairlines, not boxes.** One 1 px border per surface. Inside a surface, groups are split by a hairline or space, never by a nested card. KPIs sit in one shared strip with dividers, not four cards.
4. **Density that stays calm.** 13.5 px UI text in tables, 40 px rows, 16 px gutters, 20 px between surfaces. The first screen of every page shows the real work, not a stat wall.
5. **Shape and motion.** Radius 6 for controls, 10 for surfaces, full for avatars and chips. Count-up on KPIs (600 ms, once), sparklines draw in, skeletons match the layout, drawers slide 200 ms. All of it off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
6. **Keyboard first.** Grouped, collapsible sidebar with `G` then a letter shortcuts shown as hints, a command palette on `Cmd/Ctrl K` (pages, leads, projects), `/` focuses it.
7. **Bilingual and accessible.** Logical properties only, a check on `dir=rtl`, focus ring 2 px ember on every control, 4.5:1 text and 3:1 for chart marks and chip borders, labels on every icon button.

## Signature screens

- **Dashboard:** one KPI strip (value, delta, 6-point sparkline), funnel and trend charts, hot leads as rows with an avatar, a BANT chip (four segments, B A N T), heat word and the last WhatsApp message as a small bubble preview.
- **Leads:** filter bar (search, status segment, temperature), dense table, a right drawer with BANT breakdown, activity timeline and the conversation.
- **Messages:** conversation list on the left, a WhatsApp Business style thread (day dividers, tails, ticks), AI suggested reply chips above the composer, a Calendly card for scheduling.
- **Calendar and Reports:** the same card, chart and header treatment.
- **Mobile 375:** bottom navigation for the five main areas, a "More" sheet for the rest, stacked cards, the lead drawer as a full-height sheet, no horizontal scroll.
